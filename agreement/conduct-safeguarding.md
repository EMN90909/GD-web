# HR-002 — Code of conduct and safeguarding

**DRAFT — REVIEW BEFORE ADOPTION**

Acelden aims to be inclusive, respectful and safe for collaborators and applicants. No harassment, bullying, discrimination, threats, sexual advances under pressure, exploitation, retaliation, or abuse of authority. Maintain professional boundaries and avoid coercive personal relationships involving supervisory power.

For minors: prioritize school and safety, use age-appropriate communication, avoid private unsupervised arrangements where unsafe, do not solicit sensitive images or identity documents, and establish appropriate guardian involvement and safeguarding reporting routes. Never make employment or access conditional on personal attention.

Reports must be acknowledged confidentially, shared only on a need-to-know basis, assessed fairly, and escalated to an independent responsible adult or appropriate authority when required. Define named safeguarding contacts, response timelines, evidence retention, and emergency escalation before accepting minors into organized activities. The founders must not investigate complaints about themselves without independent oversight.
