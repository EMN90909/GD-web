# HR-001 — Employment and contributor policy

**DRAFT — NOT AN OFFER OF EMPLOYMENT**

## Classification and compensation
Distinguish employees, genuinely independent contractors, interns/trainees, and optional community participants by the real relationship, not labels. No salaries or work obligations are promised while unfunded. Do not use unpaid trials, internships or volunteer labels to evade statutory obligations. Any productive work requires a lawful arrangement, written scope, compensation terms where applicable, and IP terms.

## Written terms
Before appointment document duties, supervisor, place of work, hours, compensation and payment dates, leave, equipment, confidentiality, IP, performance process, grievances and lawful termination. Apply applicable Kenyan employment, wage, tax and statutory requirements.

## Work practices
Respect school and examinations, reasonable hours, breaks, safety and accessibility. For minors, verify age-related restrictions, permissible work, supervision and guardian involvement before any work. No hazardous or exploitative work.

## Security and IP
Use individual accounts, least-privilege access, MFA where supported, secure handling of personal data, license checks for third-party assets, and written ownership/assignment provisions. Return access and assets at departure.

## Conduct and accountability
No harassment, discrimination, sexual coercion, retaliation or bullying. Provide confidential reporting, a neutral escalation route independent of either founder, fair investigation and proportionate action. No one should be required to disclose private messages or unrelated personal information.

## Exit
Document notice, handover, access revocation, final payments and statutory entitlements. No policy overrides applicable law. Review with Kenyan counsel before implementation.
