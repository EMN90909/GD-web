# Acelden Founders and Shareholders Agreement — heads of terms

**DRAFT — NOT EXECUTED — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED (KENYA)**

## Parties and company
Proposed founders: Clyde Bradley (Founder / CEO) and Emmanuel Nasong'o (Co-founder / CTO). Record full legal identities and company registration details after verification. Proposed economic ownership is 50% each, subject to lawful incorporation, share issuance and any age/capacity restrictions. These terms do not themselves issue shares.

## Responsibilities
CEO: studio direction, business relationships, operations and production coordination. CTO: architecture, security, engineering and technical strategy. Both founders jointly approve reserved matters; ordinary operations may be delegated within an agreed budget.

## Reserved matters
Unanimous written approval required for equity changes, new shareholders, borrowing, material contracts, executive appointments, changes to founder compensation, sale of material assets/IP, significant budgets, and amendments to Noethric technology licensing. Define monetary thresholds before signing.

## Contributions and vesting
Create a dated schedule of cash, equipment, prior work, domains, assets and licenses contributed by each founder. Negotiate vesting, treatment of prior contributions, cliff, good/bad leaver events and fair valuation. Do not presume that pre-existing Noethric software transfers to Acelden.

## Deadlocks
First exchange written proposals; negotiate in good faith for 14 days; seek neutral mediation; use a specifically negotiated lawful mechanism for unresolved fundamental deadlocks. No automatic forced sale or unilateral casting vote is established by this draft.

## IP and conflicts
Acelden's original game-specific IP must be assigned under separate valid agreements. Noethric retains pre-existing NoeEngine rights and grants only rights set out in a separate license, subject to third-party open-source licenses. Manu's relationship with Noethric must be disclosed and related-party approvals documented with independent advice where appropriate.

## Financial controls and records
Maintain separate company accounts, transparent expense reporting, dual approvals for reserved spending, accurate books and conflict disclosures. Record ownership, contributions, resolutions and signed documents securely.

## Exits and disputes
Specify voluntary exit, incapacity, material breach, handover, vesting treatment, valuation, transfer restrictions, confidentiality and dispute resolution. Governing law proposed: Kenya, subject to counsel review.

## Execution conditions
Confirm founder ages, capacity, shareholding, director eligibility, company registration, constitution, tax and employment implications with qualified Kenyan counsel. Signature blocks and witness requirements to be prepared only after legal review. Nothing here is an executed contract.
