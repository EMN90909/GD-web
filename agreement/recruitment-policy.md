# REC-001 — Friends-first recruitment and vetting policy

**DRAFT — TALENT POOL ONLY / NO FUNDED OPENINGS**

## Scope
Acelden initially invites trusted friends and referrals to express interest. A referral does not guarantee selection, compensation, access, equity or employment. Target 10–15 expressions of interest, shortlist up to six, and consider activating four project roles only when funded and legally appropriate.

## Stages
1. Founder referral and candidate consent. 2. Explain purpose, privacy and no-vacancy status. 3. Review interests, existing work and realistic availability. 4. Optional brief role-specific assessment, not unpaid commercial production. 5. Both founders review consistent criteria. 6. Record talent-pool, deferred, withdrawn or declined outcome. 7. Obtain lawful agreements before any productive work.

## Evaluation
Practical skills 30%; reliability 25%; teamwork 20%; creativity 15%; learning potential 10%. Use evidence-based rubrics, accommodations and consistent role requirements. Serious safety or conduct concerns are reviewed separately. No discrimination or harassment. A gender-diverse studio is encouraged through inclusive outreach, not unequal hiring standards.

## Privacy
Collect minimal information, give a privacy notice, obtain appropriate consent, restrict access, establish retention and deletion periods, and provide withdrawal/correction channels. Never request passwords or unnecessary identity documents. The current static join page only downloads a local draft and does not collect applications.

## Minors
Age-appropriate interactions, school commitments, safe communications, appropriate guardian involvement and Kenyan child-employment/safeguarding rules must be reviewed before any role or trial. Do not treat students as free labor.

## Initial candidates
Mike and Leon may be invited to optional NoeEngine exploration exercises with clear scope and no production deliverables; their abilities and availability remain unassessed.
