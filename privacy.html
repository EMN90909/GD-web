01 / SCOPE

THE SHORT VERSION.

This policy describes the public Acelden studio website. Acelden is a teen-led game development studio in Kenya. This page does not cover external websites, future games or services that may have their own policies.

02 / TECHNICAL DATA

WHAT WE RECEIVE.

When you visit, the hosting provider may process your IP address, browser details, request times and security logs to deliver and protect the site. The website does not currently offer user accounts or profiles.

We have not deliberately added advertising trackers or a custom analytics platform to the site's code. External assets such as hosted fonts may involve requests to third parties.

03 / MESSAGES

CONTACT & ENQUIRIES.

The visible contact form is not currently connected to a working message-delivery service. Do not submit sensitive information through it. If we enable submissions, we will update this policy to describe the information collected, delivery provider, retention and deletion process before treating the form as operational.

04 / EXTERNAL SERVICES

OTHER PLATFORMS.

We link to GitHub, Instagram, Noethric and other websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Our website is hosted on Render, and external font or image providers may receive requests when their resources load.

05 / YOUR RIGHTS

YOUR INFORMATION.

You may ask about personal information you believe Acelden holds, or request correction or deletion where applicable. We will assess requests under relevant law. Hosting and security logs may be controlled by the provider and subject to its retention policies.

For privacy questions, reach us through the contact page or our linked studio social channels. Please do not include sensitive personal information in public messages.

06 / POLICY CHANGES

IF THINGS CHANGE.

We will revise this notice as the website gains new capabilities, especially working contact delivery, analytics, accounts or game-related services. We will update the date at the top when material changes are made.
