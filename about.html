Acelden is an independent game development studio based in Kenya. We believe great games start with curiosity, bold experiments, and a reason to keep playing.

01 / WHERE WE START

SMALL
BEGINNINGS.

We're starting with Roblox: a place to learn through making, experiment with gameplay loops, and build experiences people can enjoy together. The goal isn't to promise a giant universe before we've built one. It's to get the fundamentals right, then keep growing.

02 / WHERE WE'RE GOING

BIGGER
WORLDS.

Our long-term direction is original 3D games with deeper worlds, expressive art direction, and memorable interactions. We're exploring how our own tools can support that ambition, including NoeEngine, our Godot-based engine project currently in development.

03 / HOW WE BUILD

IDEAS INTO
INTERACTION.

We prototype, test, learn, and iterate. Fun comes before feature lists. We care about controls that feel responsive, worlds that invite discovery, and creative tools that let a small team work with focus.

Acelden works in partnership with Noethric, a Kenyan product studio focused on building practical technology.
