THE BEGINNING

WE'RE HERE
TO BUILD.

Acelden is an early-stage game studio founded by two friends with a shared interest in games and the worlds behind them.

We plan to begin by creating Roblox experiences and learn from real players before taking on larger 3D projects. Alongside our games, we are exploring tools that could make creating them more accessible and expressive.

We're not presenting a finished catalog or a large team. We believe being honest about where we are is part of making something worth following.
