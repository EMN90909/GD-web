THE STUDIO / OUR STORY

PLAY IS
THE POINT.

Acelden is a Kenyan game development studio founded and led by teenagers. We are building our skills, our tools and our first games, starting with Roblox and working toward original 3D experiences.

KENYA / INDEPENDENTTEEN-LED STUDIOEST. 2026
01 / WHERE WE START

SMALL
BEGINNINGS.

We are starting with Roblox. It gives us a place to build game mechanics, learn from playtesting and create experiences people can enjoy together. We are not claiming finished games before they exist. Our focus is making things worth playing.

02 / WHERE WE'RE GOING

BIGGER
WORLDS.

We want to create original 3D games with memorable environments, responsive controls and mechanics that invite exploration. NoeEngine is our engine and editor project, with Noqeri language support among the integrations we are developing.

03 / HOW WE BUILD

IDEAS INTO
INTERACTION.

We prototype, test, learn, and iterate. Fun comes before feature lists. We care about controls that feel responsive, worlds that invite discovery, and creative tools that let a small team work with focus.

Acelden is a teen-led Kenyan studio co-founded by Clyde Bradley and Emmanuel Nasong'o. We work in partnership with Noethric, a Kenyan product studio building practical technology and the Noqeri programming language.
